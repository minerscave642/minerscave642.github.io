Aiming Fundamentals

Good aim starts with crosshair placement and smooth mouse control. Keep your crosshair at head level and pre‑aim common angles.

  • Crosshair at head height
  • Pre‑aim corners
  • Use small, controlled mouse movements

Movement Techniques

Movement affects accuracy. Learn when to counter‑strafe, walk, or peek aggressively.

  • Counter‑strafe to stop instantly
  • Silent walking for sneaky plays
  • Shoulder peeking to gather info
